Title: App does not detect the charger

If the mobile app cannot find your charger during setup or shows it as offline:

Setup phase (Bluetooth):
- Confirm the charger is powered on. The LED should be lit.
- Confirm phone Bluetooth and location services are enabled. Location is required by the operating system for Bluetooth scanning.
- Move within 3 meters of the charger.
- Close and reopen the app.

Post-setup (Wi-Fi):
- Confirm the charger has an active internet connection. See the Wi-Fi troubleshooting article.
- Check whether the charger is reported offline in the cloud portal from a browser; if it is offline there too, the issue is on the charger side, not the app.
- Log out and log back in to the app to refresh the token.
- Reinstall the app if issues persist.

If the charger has been offline for more than 7 days, it may require a manual reconnection via Bluetooth to refresh the Wi-Fi credentials.
