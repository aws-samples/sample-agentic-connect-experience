Title: Resetting your account password

If you have forgotten your password or need to update it:

1. On the login screen of the app or web portal, select "Forgot password".
2. Enter the email address associated with your account and submit.
3. Check your inbox for a password reset link. The link is valid for 30 minutes.
4. Follow the link and choose a new password of at least 10 characters with one uppercase letter, one number, and one symbol.
5. Log in with the new password.

If you no longer have access to the email address associated with the account, you must contact support with proof of ownership of the charger, such as the serial number and the original purchase invoice. Support cannot reset a password without verifying account ownership.

For security, changing the password logs out all active sessions on other devices.
