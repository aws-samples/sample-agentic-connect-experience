Title: Adding a charger to your account

Every charger must be linked to a user account before it can be controlled remotely or receive firmware updates.

Steps to add a charger:
1. Log in to the app.
2. From the main screen, select "Add charger".
3. Choose the model of your charger from the list.
4. Enter the 8-character serial number, which is printed on a sticker on the side of the unit and on the packaging.
5. The app will attempt to connect over Bluetooth. Make sure the charger is powered on and you are within 3 meters of the unit.
6. Follow the guided setup to connect the charger to your Wi-Fi network.
7. Assign a name to the charger for easier identification.

A charger can only be linked to one account at a time. If the charger was previously used by a different owner, you must ask the previous owner to unlink it, or contact support with proof of purchase to have the previous link released.
