Title: Cable and connector issues

Cables and connectors are wear items and can degrade over time. Symptoms of a failing cable:

- The vehicle does not detect that the cable is plugged in.
- Intermittent charging sessions that pause and resume without an error code.
- Visible damage to the outer jacket, exposed conductors, or bent pins on the connector.
- The connector feels unusually warm after a session (some warmth is normal; excessive heat is not).
- The cable lock does not engage or release properly.

Do not use a cable that shows visible damage. Damaged cables are a shock and fire hazard.

For tethered chargers, replacement cables must match the original power rating and connector type (Type 2 in Europe, Type 1 or NACS in North America depending on the model). Only certified replacement parts should be used. Contact support to order a replacement.

For untethered chargers (socket only), any certified Mode 3 cable of the correct current rating can be used.
