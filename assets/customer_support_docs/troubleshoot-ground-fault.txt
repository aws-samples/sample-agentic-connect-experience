Title: Ground fault error (E002 / E009)

Ground fault errors indicate that current is leaking to ground through a path other than the intended circuit. This is a safety-critical condition and charging is disabled until resolved.

Do not attempt to bypass the fault detection or repeatedly reset the RCD. Ground faults can indicate wet connectors, damaged insulation, or a fault in the vehicle's charging system.

Steps to diagnose:
1. Disconnect the cable from the vehicle and inspect both connectors for moisture, dirt, or damage.
2. Wipe connectors with a dry cloth. Never use water or solvents.
3. Wait 5 minutes and reset the RCD in the electrical panel.
4. Attempt a session with a different vehicle if available. If the fault only occurs with one vehicle, the vehicle likely has an internal issue and should be checked at a service center.
5. If the fault occurs regardless of the vehicle, contact a certified electrician to inspect the installation and the charger.

Repeated ground faults during rain may indicate that the charger is not correctly rated for its outdoor exposure. Verify the IP rating matches the installation environment.
