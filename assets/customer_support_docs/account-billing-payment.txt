Title: Managing billing and payment methods

Subscription plans and public charging session fees are charged to the payment method registered on your account.

To add or update a payment method:
1. Log in to the app or web portal.
2. Go to "Account", then "Billing and payments".
3. Select "Add payment method" or edit an existing one.
4. Enter the card details. Supported cards are Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. Some regions also support SEPA direct debit.
5. Confirm with 3D Secure if prompted by your bank.

Invoices are issued monthly and are available under "Billing history" as downloadable PDFs. Failed payments trigger a retry after 3 days. After 2 failed retries, the subscription enters a suspended state and premium features become unavailable until the balance is settled.

Refunds for public charging sessions can be requested within 60 days of the transaction from the "Session history" screen.
