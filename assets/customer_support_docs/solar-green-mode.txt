Title: Solar and green energy charging modes

If your installation includes solar panels, the charger can prioritize charging from surplus solar production.

Available modes:
- Fast: charges at maximum power regardless of solar production, drawing from grid as needed.
- Eco: charges using a mix of solar and grid, but only when solar contribution is above a configurable percentage.
- Full solar: charges only from surplus solar production. If solar drops below a threshold (typically 1.4 kW single-phase), the session pauses until sufficient production returns.
- Scheduled: charges at defined times regardless of solar.

Configuration:
1. A compatible energy meter or inverter integration must be installed and detected by the charger.
2. In the app, go to "Charger settings", then "Solar and green".
3. Select the desired mode and configure thresholds.
4. Confirm that the meter is reading correctly by comparing values to the inverter or utility meter.

Full solar mode may result in longer overall session times, especially during winter or cloudy days. Consider Eco mode as a balance between cost and completion time.
