Title: Creating and activating your account

To use the mobile app and cloud portal for your EV charger, you need an active user account.

Steps to create an account:
1. Download the mobile app from the App Store or Google Play, or open the web portal.
2. Select "Sign up" and enter your email address and a password of at least 10 characters, including one uppercase letter, one number, and one symbol.
3. Accept the terms of service and privacy policy.
4. Check your email for a verification message. Click the link within 24 hours to activate the account. If the link expires, request a new one from the login screen.
5. After verification, log in and complete your profile with your country of residence and preferred language.

If you do not receive the verification email within 10 minutes, check your spam folder and confirm that the address is spelled correctly. Corporate email filters may block automated messages; if this is the case, use a personal address or contact your IT department to allowlist the sender domain.
