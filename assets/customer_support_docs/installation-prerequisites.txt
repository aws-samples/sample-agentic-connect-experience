Title: Installation prerequisites

Chargers must be installed by a certified electrician. Attempting to install the unit yourself voids the warranty and may violate local electrical codes.

Before installation, confirm:
- A dedicated circuit is available. Do not share the charger circuit with other appliances.
- The circuit breaker rating matches the charger model (typically 16A, 32A, or 40A).
- The RCD (Type A EV or Type B) is installed, per local regulations.
- The installation location is within cable reach of the vehicle's charging port when parked normally.
- For outdoor installations, the mounting surface allows for proper drainage away from the unit.
- The mains supply voltage matches the charger rating (230V single-phase or 400V three-phase).

The installer will need:
- The installation manual specific to the charger model.
- Access to the electrical panel and any relevant permits.
- A network connection (Wi-Fi or Ethernet) for the initial cloud configuration.

After installation, the electrician should provide a completion certificate that includes the earth loop impedance measurement and the insulation resistance test result.
