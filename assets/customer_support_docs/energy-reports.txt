Title: Energy consumption reports

The app provides detailed reports on energy delivered, session durations, and estimated costs.

To view reports:
1. Open the app and select the charger.
2. Go to "Sessions" or "Energy".
3. Choose a time range: last 24 hours, week, month, or custom.
4. The report shows total energy in kWh, session count, average session duration, and cost estimate based on the configured tariff.

Exporting data:
Reports can be exported as CSV from the web portal for use in spreadsheets or accounting software. The CSV includes per-session detail: start time, end time, energy in kWh, user, and cost.

Configuring your tariff:
1. Go to "Account", then "Tariff".
2. Enter your energy price per kWh, and optionally define peak and off-peak periods for time-of-use pricing.
3. Cost estimates in reports will use these values.

Cost estimates are calculated from configured tariffs and do not reflect the actual utility bill, which may include additional fixed charges and taxes.
