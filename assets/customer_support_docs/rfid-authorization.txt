Title: RFID card authorization

For chargers configured to require authorization, an RFID card must be presented before a session starts. This is common in fleet, workplace, and shared parking installations.

To enroll an RFID card:
1. Go to "Charger settings", then "Access control".
2. Select "Add RFID card".
3. Present the card to the reader on the charger within 30 seconds.
4. The app confirms enrollment and lets you assign the card to a specific user.

To use an enrolled card:
1. Plug in the vehicle.
2. Present the card to the reader area (usually indicated by a card icon on the housing).
3. The LED will briefly flash green to confirm and the session will start.

Cards can be revoked at any time from the same menu. Revoking a card takes effect immediately for connected chargers and within a few minutes for chargers that have lost internet connectivity, once they reconnect.

If a card is not recognized, verify that it uses a compatible MIFARE Classic or MIFARE DESFire standard.
