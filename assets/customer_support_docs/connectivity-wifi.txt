Title: Wi-Fi connectivity troubleshooting

The charger connects to your home Wi-Fi to receive commands, report status, and download firmware updates.

Requirements:
- 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network. 5 GHz-only networks are not supported.
- WPA2 or WPA3 encryption. Open networks are not supported for security reasons.
- Signal strength of at least -70 dBm at the charger location. Weaker signals cause frequent disconnections.

If the charger is offline:
1. Verify that the router is powered on and other devices can reach the internet.
2. Restart the router and wait 2 minutes.
3. In the app, go to "Charger settings", "Network", and check the reported signal strength.
4. If the signal is weak, consider a Wi-Fi extender or moving the router closer to the charger.
5. If the network SSID or password has changed, re-provision the charger by removing it from the app and adding it again with the new credentials.

Firewalls should allow outbound TCP 443 (HTTPS) and MQTT over TLS on port 8883.
