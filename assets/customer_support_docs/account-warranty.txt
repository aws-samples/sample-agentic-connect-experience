Title: Warranty registration and coverage

Standard warranty covers 3 years on the charger unit and 2 years on the cable, starting from the date of purchase.

To register your warranty:
1. Log in to the app.
2. Go to "Account", then "My chargers".
3. Select the charger and choose "Register warranty".
4. Upload a copy of the purchase invoice showing the date, retailer, and serial number.
5. The warranty status will update to "Registered" within 3 business days.

Warranty does not cover:
- Damage from improper installation by a non-certified electrician.
- Damage from voltage surges when no surge protection is present in the installation.
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect function.
- Consumable parts such as gaskets after normal wear.

Extended warranty options up to 5 years are available for purchase within the first 12 months from the date of activation.
