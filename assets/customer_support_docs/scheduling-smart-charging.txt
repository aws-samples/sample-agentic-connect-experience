Title: Scheduling and off-peak charging

Charging sessions can be scheduled to run during off-peak electricity hours to reduce cost.

To create a schedule:
1. In the app, go to "Charger settings", then "Schedule".
2. Select "Add schedule" and choose the days of the week.
3. Enter the start and end times of the allowed charging window.
4. Optionally set a maximum power for the schedule.
5. Save. The charger will only deliver power during the configured window.

If the vehicle is plugged in outside the scheduled window, the charger enters "waiting for schedule" state (pulsing white) and starts automatically when the window opens.

Immediate charging override:
Press and hold the button on the front of the charger for 3 seconds, or use the "Charge now" button in the app to bypass the schedule for the current session. The next session will follow the schedule again.

Multiple schedules can be defined and prioritized. Overlapping schedules apply the most permissive rule.
