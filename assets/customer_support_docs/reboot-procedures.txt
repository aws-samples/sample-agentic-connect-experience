Title: Rebooting the charger

A reboot resolves many transient issues without affecting configuration or session history.

Soft reboot (from the app):
1. Open the app and select the charger.
2. Go to "Charger settings", then "Advanced".
3. Select "Restart charger" and confirm.
4. The charger will restart within 30 seconds and be available again after about 90 seconds.

Hard reboot (via power):
1. Locate the dedicated circuit breaker for the charger in the electrical panel.
2. Switch the breaker off.
3. Wait at least 60 seconds. This ensures internal capacitors discharge.
4. Switch the breaker back on.

Factory reset:
Only perform a factory reset if instructed by support. A factory reset erases Wi-Fi credentials, user pairings, and local configuration. The charger will need to be re-added to your account after a factory reset.
