Title: LED status indicator reference

The front LED ring communicates the current state of the charger. Colors and patterns:

- Solid white: idle, ready to charge, no vehicle connected.
- Pulsing white: vehicle connected, waiting for authorization or scheduled start.
- Solid green: charging in progress.
- Pulsing green: charging paused by the vehicle or by a smart charging schedule.
- Solid blue: charging session complete, vehicle still connected.
- Solid yellow: firmware update in progress. Do not disconnect power.
- Pulsing yellow: warning condition, such as reduced power due to temperature or grid limits.
- Solid red: error state. Check the app for the specific error code.
- Pulsing red: safety fault, such as ground fault or over-current. Charging is disabled until the fault is cleared.
- Off with no other indication: no power to the unit. Check the circuit breaker.

The LED brightness can be adjusted in "Charger settings", "Display", including a night mode that dims automatically at a configurable time.
