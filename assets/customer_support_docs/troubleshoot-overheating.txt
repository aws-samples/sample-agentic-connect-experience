Title: Overheating and thermal throttling

When the internal temperature of the charger exceeds safe limits, it will reduce output power or pause the session. This is normal protective behavior.

Common causes:
- Direct exposure to sunlight in high ambient temperatures.
- Installation in an enclosed space without airflow, such as a small closet.
- Blocked ventilation slots on the housing.
- Prolonged charging at maximum power on a hot day.

Mitigation:
- Install a sun shade or relocate the charger to a shaded area.
- Ensure at least 30 cm of clearance around the top and sides of the unit.
- Clean any dust from the ventilation slots with compressed air (never insert objects).
- For garages, consider passive ventilation.

The charger will resume full power automatically when the temperature drops. No user action is required to reset a thermal throttle. If the yellow warning LED persists for more than 30 minutes on a cool day, contact support.
