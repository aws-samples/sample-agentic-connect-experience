Title: Charging slower than expected

Charging speed depends on the charger, the vehicle, and the installation. Reasons a session may be slower than the rated maximum:

- The vehicle has a lower on-board charger capacity than the wall charger. Check the vehicle documentation for its AC charging limit.
- The battery state of charge is above 80 percent. Most EVs slow down charging near full to protect the battery.
- The battery or ambient temperature is low. Cold batteries accept less power until they warm up.
- The installation is dynamic load-managed and the household is drawing significant power from other appliances.
- The charger is throttling due to high internal temperature. Ensure adequate ventilation and no direct sunlight.
- A scheduled charging profile is limiting the output power during off-peak hours.

To confirm the maximum achievable rate, run a session while other loads are minimized and the battery is between 20 and 60 percent state of charge.
