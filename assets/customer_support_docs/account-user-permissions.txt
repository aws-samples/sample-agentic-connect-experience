Title: Sharing charger access with other users

You can grant access to your charger to family members, tenants, or fleet drivers.

Access roles:
- Owner: full control, including transferring ownership and deleting the charger.
- Admin: can start and stop sessions, change configuration, and manage other users, but cannot transfer ownership.
- User: can start and stop sessions and view their own session history.
- Guest: single-session access via a temporary link, no account required.

To invite a user:
1. Go to "Charger settings", then "Users and access".
2. Select "Invite user" and enter the email address.
3. Choose the role and, if applicable, a validity period.
4. The invited user receives an email with an activation link.

Owners can revoke access at any time. Session history remains attributed to the user who started the session, even after their access is revoked.
