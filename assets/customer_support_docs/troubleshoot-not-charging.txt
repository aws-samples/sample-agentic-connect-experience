Title: Charger not starting a session

If you plug in the vehicle and no charging session begins, follow these steps in order:

1. Check the LED color. Red or pulsing red indicates a fault; consult the error codes reference.
2. Verify that the vehicle is actually requesting to charge. Some vehicles have a scheduled charging feature that blocks immediate sessions.
3. Check the app for a scheduled charging window that may be delaying the start.
4. If the charger requires authorization (RFID card or app), confirm that authorization has been provided.
5. Verify that the load management system is not throttling power to zero. In multi-charger installations, an overloaded panel can pause new sessions.
6. Unplug the cable, wait 30 seconds, and reconnect firmly. A loose connection is a common cause.
7. Try charging a different vehicle to determine whether the issue is with the charger or the vehicle.

If none of these resolve the issue, capture the timestamp and error code and contact support.
