Title: Bluetooth pairing

Bluetooth is used during the initial setup to configure Wi-Fi credentials and for local control when the internet is unavailable.

To pair over Bluetooth:
1. Make sure Bluetooth is enabled on your phone.
2. Open the app and select "Add charger" or "Reconnect".
3. Bring the phone within 3 meters of the charger.
4. When prompted, confirm the 6-digit code shown on both the app and the LED ring blink pattern.

Common issues:
- Phone does not detect the charger: force-close and reopen the app, or toggle Bluetooth off and on.
- Pairing code does not match: someone else may be pairing with a nearby charger. Cancel and retry.
- Frequent disconnections: check that no other Bluetooth device is connected to the charger. The charger supports one Bluetooth session at a time.

Bluetooth pairing is only required for setup and diagnostics; day-to-day operation happens over Wi-Fi.
